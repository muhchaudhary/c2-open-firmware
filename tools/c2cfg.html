Connect the pad

Steam mode (USB, Bluetooth or the C2 receiver): Connect (Steam mode) and pick “Cyclone 2 SI” or “GameSir C2 receiver”.

Any mode, the pad on its USB cable or the receiver plugged in: Connect (log port) and pick its serial port. Close tools/c2log.py first: only one program can read the port. On Linux your user needs the dialout (or uucp) group.
